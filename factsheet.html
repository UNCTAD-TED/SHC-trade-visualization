01
Origin

It starts in the United States

Every year, households and charities across the United States set aside used clothing. It is collected, compressed into bales and prepared for export.

02
Two-stage sorting

Re-graded in Pakistan, Malaysia & UAE

Most bales travel first through sorting hubs in Pakistan, Malaysia and the UAE, where they are opened and sorted in two stages, by quality grade and by garment type, before moving on.

03
Destination

On to East African markets

Graded clothing then flows to traders in Uganda and the United Republic of Tanzania, where it is sold across busy second-hand markets.

04
Scale

Measured in millions of kilos

In a single year Uganda took in 80.0M kg and the United Republic of Tanzania 86.3M kg of secondhand clothing. Of that, only 2.0M kg and 8.0M kg arrived directly from the United States.

04
Import market share by origin

The China shift, 2018 → 2023

In just five years, China overtook the United States as the leading origin of secondhand clothing for both markets, supplying close to half of Uganda’s and more than half of Tanzania’s imports by 2023.
